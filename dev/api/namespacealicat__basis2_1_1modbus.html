◆ InterFrameGapUs()

constexpr uint32_t alicat_basis2::modbus::InterFrameGapUs ( uint32_t  bps)
constexprnoexcept

Minimum idle time t3.5 between two Modbus-RTU frames, in microseconds.

3.5 character times of 11 bits at bps, rounded up; a fixed 1750 us above 19200 baud (Modbus over Serial Line V1.02, section 2.5.1.1). bps == 0 is treated as "unknown" and gets the 4800-baud (longest) gap.
